## What this is

Rhombiverse is a free, single-player, browser-based building toy. There
is no account system, no server-side storage, and (at this stage) no
way for your content to be seen by anyone else. Everything you build is
saved only in your own browser's local storage, or in a JSON file you
explicitly export.

## Use at your own risk

The app is provided "as is," with no warranty of any kind, express or
implied — including no guarantee of uptime, data durability, or
fitness for any particular purpose. Your browser's local storage can be
cleared by you, your browser, or your OS at any time; export JSON
regularly if you want to keep a build.

## Acceptable use

Don't attempt to disrupt, attack, or scrape the hosting infrastructure.
Don't use the app for anything unlawful. There is no multiplayer or
shared content yet, so there is currently nothing to abuse toward other
users — this section exists for when that changes.

## Your content

Anything you build stays local to your own browser or your own
exported JSON files; the app does not transmit it anywhere. If a future
version adds shared/multiplayer worlds (Phase 5+), these terms will be
updated to cover content ownership and hosting rights before that
ships.

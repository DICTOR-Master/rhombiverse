# Privacy Policy

Effective for the public playable link described in `RHOMBIVERSE_PLAN.md`
Phase 4.

## Short version

Rhombiverse does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on
a server. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no cookies. The only
data that exists is whatever you build, and it stays in your own
browser.

## What data exists, and where

- **World data** (the cells you place, their materials, undo history)
  is stored only in your browser's `localStorage`, on your own device.
  It is never sent to any server — this app has no backend to send it
  to.
- **Exported JSON files** are created and downloaded locally when you
  use "Export JSON"; they are not uploaded anywhere by the app.
- **Standard web server logs.** The static hosting provider (see below)
  may log ordinary access data (IP address, timestamp, requested file)
  as part of normal web hosting operation — the app itself has no
  access to or control over this.

## Third parties

The app is served as static files from a hosting provider (Vercel).
That provider's own privacy policy governs any hosting-level logs
generated by serving the page — this project does not add any
additional tracking, analytics, or third-party scripts on top of that.

## Data deletion

Since nothing is stored server-side, there is nothing to request
deletion of beyond your own browser: clearing this site's data in your
browser settings removes everything.

## Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children. If a future version adds accounts or shared
worlds, this policy will be revisited with real legal review before
that ships (see `docs/RHOMBIVERSE_COMPLIANCE.md`'s COPPA note).

## Changes

If a future phase adds a backend, accounts, or analytics, this policy
will be updated to disclose exactly what's collected and why, before
that phase ships.
